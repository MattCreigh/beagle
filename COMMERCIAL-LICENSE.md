work remain free under LICENSE-NONCOMMERCIAL.

## The optional beagle-orpheus wheel

The proprietary `beagle-orpheus` transport wheel is licensed separately
from Beagle itself under its own agreement:

- **Evaluation / limited single use:** free — evaluate the native
  FlatBuffers-over-ring-buffer transport in development and trials.
- **Production / business deployment:** requires a paid beagle-orpheus
  license. Installing the wheel constitutes acceptance of that agreement.

beagle functions fully without this component on the built-in HTTP
transport; installing it is always an explicit operator decision.
